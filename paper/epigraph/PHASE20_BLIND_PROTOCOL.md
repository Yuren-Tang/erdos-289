# Phase I.10 blind plaque-field micro-grid

Purpose: correct the direction of the final vertical-spacing experiment. The epigraph is treated as an independent plaque-like page element, but any optical compensation is now constrained so that the upper white space is never larger than the lower white space.

## Fixed parameters

- family/weight: EB Garamond Medium;
- tracking: `LetterSpace=9`;
- wording: `CARPE DIEM QVAM MINIMVM CREDVLA POSTERO`;
- punctuation: none;
- size/leading: 10.5/16;
- ordinary OpenType kerning; no hand kerning or synthetic emboldening.

## Micro-grid

Two variables are tested independently over a deliberately narrow range:

1. lower (`post`) space: 12, 13, or 14 pt;
2. upper (`pre`) compensation relative to the lower space: 0, -0.5, or -1 pt.

Hence the nine source proofs are:

- 11/12, 11.5/12, 12/12;
- 12/13, 12.5/13, 13/13;
- 13/14, 13.5/14, 14/14.

This directly reflects the Owner's correction after Phase I.9: if an asymmetry is useful at all, it should be slight and should make the upper space *smaller*, not larger. The lower-space axis is retained because, when judged independently of the incorrect upper-spacing direction, the wider lower gaps were more comfortable.

## Blind packet

The judgement packet contains all nine first pages in concealed randomized order. No parameter labels are shown before judgement.

The concealed key, revealed only after the Owner's ranking was frozen, was:

```text
page 1 = 12.5/13
page 2 = 12/13
page 3 = 11.5/12
page 4 = 13.5/14
page 5 = 13/13
page 6 = 12/12
page 7 = 13/14
page 8 = 14/14
page 9 = 11/12
```

## Blind result

The Owner reported, before seeing the key:

- page 2 better than pages 1 and 3;
- page 4 better than page 5, better than page 6;
- pages 2 and 4 approximately tied, with a slight preference for page 2;
- page 9 better than page 7, better than page 8;
- pages 9 and 2 approximately tied, with a slight preference for page 9;
- all fine distinctions were explicitly regarded as potentially session-dependent perceptual noise.

Thus the leading observed sequence was approximately

```text
11/12  >  12/13  ≳  13.5/14,
```

with only small optical margins. The most important structural point is that the blind winner `11/12` exactly preserves the already-comfortable 12 pt lower gap while increasing the upper gap from the original 8 pt to 11 pt, still leaving the upper field 1 pt smaller than the lower field. This matches the intended plaque model without forcing numeric symmetry.

Because the Owner explicitly warned that these sub-point/few-point preferences may change on a later viewing, Phase I.10 establishes **11/12 as the provisional vertical-spacing leader**, not yet as a mathematically precise optical optimum. Further work, if any, should be confirmatory rather than a finer parameter search; do not chase tenths of a point.

No publication-manuscript change is authorized by this experiment.
