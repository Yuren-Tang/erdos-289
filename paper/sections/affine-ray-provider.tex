\section{The source-closed affine-ray provider}
\label{sec:affine-provider}

The root needs an affine family and the later Tail response at the \emph{same}
compact source.  Finiteness of an affine footprint at each fixed stage is not
enough: the footprint must be uniformly negligible as the source itself moves.
We therefore use the source-closed Prefix certificate of
Appendix~\ref{app:source-closed-prefix}.

For a numerical endpoint index $B$, write
\[
E_B=\bigvee_{Q\le B}J_Q
=H_{\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,B)}.
\]
Appendix~\ref{app:source-closed-prefix} gives fixed constants
\[
\eta_0>0
\]
and, for every sufficiently large $B$, a finite homogeneous physical family
$P_B$ together with a quotient point
\[
a_B\in A/E_B
\]
such that the residue map is a regular epimorphism
\[
\operatorname{res}:P_B\twoheadrightarrow q_{E_B}^{-1}(a_B),
\tag{A0}\label{eq:prefix-coset-cover}
\]
every branch has reciprocal value in $(0,2-\eta_0)$, and the complete Prefix
family satisfies the uniform envelope estimate required in
Lemma~\ref{lem:sparse-survival}.  In particular, after increasing $B$ once,
$P_B$ is a $c_0$-transparent obstacle at the same source $E_B$ for one fixed
$c_0>0$, including all boundary-predecessor rows.

The fixed finite root, lcm coordinates and bridge coefficients used to prove
\eqref{eq:prefix-coset-cover} have no consumer above the appendix.

Write
\[
e_2=(1,0),
\qquad
e_3=(0,1)
\]
for the generators of the component-profile monoid $\N^2$.

\subsection{Equal-value defect translation}

Put
\[
P(n)=\frac1n+\frac1{n+1}.
\]
For every even $a\ge4$, define
\[
b=\frac{a(a+2)}2-2.
\]
Then
\[
P(a)-P(a+1)
=
\frac1a-\frac1{a+2}
=
\frac1{b+2}.
\]
Therefore the two physical states
\[
[a,a+1]\sqcup[b,b+1]
\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
[a+1,a+2]\sqcup[b,b+2]
\tag{A1}\label{eq:defect-switch}
\]
have equal reciprocal value and equal total grade two, while their component
profiles are
\[
2e_2
\quad\text{and}\quad
e_2+e_3.
\]
Thus one high alternative translates the profile by $e_3-e_2$ without
changing exact value or total grade.

As $a\to\infty$, the complete two-state footprint moves arbitrarily far out
and its common reciprocal mass tends to zero.  Finite collections of these
coordinates may therefore be constructed successively against any prescribed
finite physical constraint, with arbitrarily small total common mass.

\subsection{Defect filling by a cardinality pullback}

Fix one sufficiently large source $B$ and its finite homogeneous Prefix family
$P_B$.  Define
\[
d_0(B)=\max_{x\in P_B}b_3(x).
\tag{A2}\label{eq:defect-floor}
\]
Let $L\ge d_0(B)$.  Construct $L$ mutually compatible remote copies of
\eqref{eq:defect-switch}, choosing their positive common masses so that the
total is smaller than $\eta_0/2$.

For a Prefix state $x$ and a requested defect
\[
d\in[d_0(B),L],
\]
we have
\[
0\le d-b_3(x)\le L.
\]
Pull the cardinality map
\[
\operatorname{card}:
\operatorname{FinSub}(\{1,\ldots,L\})
\longrightarrow
\{0,\ldots,L\}
\]
back along
\[
(x,d)\longmapsto d-b_3(x).
\]
The cardinality map is surjective, so this selector object projects regularly
epimorphically to the Prefix-state/request pair.  Selecting the high
alternative on a subset of cardinality $d-b_3(x)$ raises the ternary count to
exactly $d$ while preserving reciprocal value and total grade.

Let $\omega_{B,L}$ be the sum of the common reciprocal values of the $L$
defect coordinates.  It is independent of every selector choice.  Define
\[
a_{B,L}=a_B+q_{E_B}([\omega_{B,L}])\in A/E_B.
\]
The selector construction then gives a literal map
\[
(\operatorname{res},b_3):
S_{B,L}\longrightarrow
q_{E_B}^{-1}(a_{B,L})\times[d_0(B),L].
\]

\begin{lemma}[Concrete MasterSlab coset coverage]
\label{lem:masterslab-coset}
The preceding map is a regular epimorphism:
\[
(\operatorname{res},b_3):
S_{B,L}\twoheadrightarrow
q_{E_B}^{-1}(a_{B,L})\times[d_0(B),L].
\tag{A3}\label{eq:masterslab-cover}
\]
All branches of $S_{B,L}$ have one common total grade.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Take a target pair $(y,d)$ on the right.  Since
$y-[\omega_{B,L}]$ lies in $q_{E_B}^{-1}(a_B)$,
\eqref{eq:prefix-coset-cover} has a Prefix realizer $x$ with that residue.
Now $0\le d-b_3(x)\le L$.  Pullback-surjectivity of the cardinality map gives
the full nonempty fibre of subsets of cardinality $d-b_3(x)$.  For every such
selector, exactly one alternative is taken in each defect coordinate, so the
added reciprocal value is the common quantity $\omega_{B,L}$; hence the final
residue is $y$ and the final ternary count is $d$.  This proves surjectivity.

Every Prefix branch has one common grade, and each defect coordinate has grade
two on both alternatives.  Thus every final branch receives the same grade
increment $2L$.
\end{proof}

Since the defect coordinates add total reciprocal mass less than $\eta_0/2$,
all slab branches lie in
\[
(0,2-\eta),
\qquad
\eta:=\eta_0/2>0,
\tag{A4}\label{eq:slab-margin}
\]
uniformly in $B$ and $L$.

\subsection{Uniform transparency of the defect cube}

The Prefix itself has the uniform moving-window estimate of
Appendix~\ref{app:source-closed-prefix}.  It remains to ensure that the number
of defect coordinates does not destroy that estimate.

Construct the defect coordinates so that the first vertex of each new complete
envelope lies beyond the square of the maximum vertex used previously.  A late
Tail current $Q$ has the fixed current window $I_Q$ of
\eqref{eq:tail-current-window}.  If two different defect coordinates met the
same sufficiently late window, the later one would begin beyond the square of
a quantity $\gg Q^2/\log Q$ while simultaneously lying $O(Q^2)$, which is
impossible.  Hence at most one defect coordinate meets any sufficiently late
Tail window, uniformly in $L$.

Each coordinate has a fixed finite number of component envelopes.  Thus the
whole defect cube adds only $O(1)$ to the Prefix-window incidence bound,
uniformly in both $B$ and $L$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:sparse-survival} therefore gives
one fixed $c>0$ such that every sufficiently late MasterSlab is
$c$-transparent at its own source $E_B$, including boundary predecessors.

\begin{theorem}[Source-closed MasterSlab]\label{thm:masterslab}
There exist fixed constants $c>0$ and $\eta>0$ and an index $B_0$ such that
for every $B\ge B_0$ and every $L\ge d_0(B)$ there is a finite homogeneous
family $S_{B,L}$ for which \eqref{eq:masterslab-cover} is regular epi, every
branch has reciprocal value in $(0,2-\eta)$, and the complete family is
$c$-transparent for the Tail at the same source $E_B$.
\end{theorem}

The filtered affine ray used in Section~\ref{sec:germs} is obtained by setting
$L=d$ and pulling \eqref{eq:masterslab-cover} back along the point
$\{d\}\hookrightarrow[d_0(B),d]$.  No abstract correction-requirement object
is consumed above this section.
