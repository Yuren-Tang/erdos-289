\section{Terminal pullback and exact reciprocal sum}
\label{sec:terminalization}

Fix a sufficiently large integer $k$.  By Theorem~\ref{thm:scalar-terminality}, choose
\[
X\in\mathcal X_k.
\]
Then the image $\gamma_X$ of the affine centre is zero in $A/E_X$ and $k\in I_X$.  Hence
\[
(0,k)\in T_X
=\operatorname{Tor}_{E_X}(\gamma_X)\times I_X.
\]

By Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-realization}, choose a covering observable labelled relation
\[
R_X:\{0_M\}\rightsquigarrow T_X
\]
all of whose edge labels have reciprocal value below $2$.  Let
\[
\tau_X:R_X\longrightarrow T_X
\]
be the target projection.  Pull it back along the point $(0,k):\{*\}\to T_X$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:terminal-edge-pullback}
\begin{CD}
E_{X,k} @>>> R_X\\
@VVV @VV{\tau_X}V\\
\{*\} @>{(0,k)}>> T_X.
\end{CD}
\end{equation}
Since $\tau_X$ is surjective, the left vertical map is surjective, so $E_{X,k}$ is nonempty.  Choose an edge
\[
(0_M,S,(0,k))\in R_X
\]
lying over the point $(0,k)$.  The conservation law \eqref{eq:edge-conservation} gives
\[
(0,k)=0_M+\Omega(S)=\bigl(\overline W(S),\nu(S)\bigr).
\]
Together with the reciprocal-mass bound on $R_X$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:terminal-state}
\overline W(S)=0,
\qquad
\nu(S)=k,
\qquad
W(S)<2.
\end{equation}

Let
\[
q_{\Z}:\Q\longrightarrow\Q/\Z
\]
be the quotient map.  By definition of $\overline W$, the square
\begin{equation}\label{eq:value-residue-square}
\begin{CD}
\mathcal C @>{W}>> \Q\\
@V{\overline W}VV @VV{q_{\Z}}V\\
\Q/\Z @= \Q/\Z
\end{CD}
\end{equation}
commutes.  Hence $\overline W(S)=0$ implies
\[
W(S)\in\ker q_{\Z}=\Z.
\]
Since $\nu(S)=k>0$, the configuration is nonempty and $W(S)>0$.  Together with \eqref{eq:terminal-state},
\[
0<W(S)<2,
\qquad
W(S)\in\Z,
\]
so $W(S)=1$.  The $k$ connected components of $S$ are pairwise nonadjacent intervals of cardinality $2$ or $3$.  This proves Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
