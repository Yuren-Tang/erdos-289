% Source-filtered root exactification.

\section{Filtered response germs and exactification}
\label{sec:germs}

The shortest root consumes two source-filtered responses:

\begin{itemize}
\item the filtered affine ray, obtained by restricting the stronger
MasterSlab provider to one defect fibre;
\item the direct late-source targeted Tail response of
Theorem~\ref{thm:ltar-closed}.
\end{itemize}

The Tail response is already target-specific.  The historical full BinaryTail
cover, compact request and least absorber are therefore library mathematics,
not nodes of the active exactification.

\subsection{Projection of MasterSlab to a filtered affine ray}

Let $(B,\mathcal F_B)$ be the Prefix base and
\[
\beta:B\to S_R
\]
its Tail-source map.  The affine provider proves
\[
\operatorname{Tendsto}(\beta,\mathcal F_B,\mathcal F_R).
\tag{F1}\label{eq:affine-tendsto-root}
\]
Over uniform generalized certificates $(c,\eta)$, eventually in $b$ and for
every
\[
L\ge d_0(b)
\]
there is a normalized finite family $S_{b,L}$ for which
\[
(\operatorname{required},b_3):
S_{b,L}\twoheadrightarrow
\operatorname{Required}(\phi_{b,L})\times[d_0(b),L]
\tag{F2}\label{eq:slab-root}
\]
is regular epi, all branches have one common total grade, branch value lies in
$(0,2-\eta)$, and the complete family is $c$-transparent for the later Tail
rows.

\begin{proposition}[Affine-ray fibre projection]
\label{prop:affine-ray-projection}
For every sufficiently late $b$ and every $d\ge d_0(b)$ there is a finite
normalized physical family
\[
F_{b,d}\twoheadrightarrow q_{H_b}^{-1}(a_{b,d})
\]
covering one literal affine coset in $A=\Q/\Z$, with constant component profile
\[
(m_{b,d},d),
\]
branch value in $(0,2-\eta)$, and the same transparency lower bound $c$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In \eqref{eq:slab-root} substitute $L=d$ and pull back along the terminal defect
fibre
\[
\operatorname{Required}(\phi_{b,d})\times\{d\}
\hookrightarrow
\operatorname{Required}(\phi_{b,d})\times[d_0(b),d].
\]
Pullback stability of regular epimorphisms gives a cover of
$\operatorname{Required}(\phi_{b,d})$.  Since the affine correction starts at
the zero compact stage and ends at $H_b$, this required object is the literal
coset
\[
q_{H_b}^{-1}(a_{b,d})\subseteq A.
\]
The fibre has exact ternary count $d$ and inherits the common total grade
$K_{b,d}$, hence its component profile is
\[
(K_{b,d}-d,d).
\]
The branchwise margin and transparency restrict to the fibre.  Every datum
belonging only to the other defect fibres has reached its last consumer and is
forgotten by regular image.
\end{proof}

The family of Proposition~\ref{prop:affine-ray-projection}, together with
\eqref{eq:affine-tendsto-root}, is the filtered affine ray.

\subsection{Meeting the source germs and freezing one source}

Use the affine margin to set the canonical Tail tolerance
\[
\varepsilon=\eta/2.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:ltar-closed} supplies a $\mathcal F_R$-large locus
$U_{\rm tail}$ on which this tolerance works uniformly for transparent finite
obstacles and target residues.  By \eqref{eq:affine-tendsto-root}, its inverse
image is $\mathcal F_B$-large.  Intersect it with the eventual affine-valid
locus:
\[
U_{\rm meet}
=
U_{\rm aff}\cap\beta^{-1}(U_{\rm tail})
\in\mathcal F_B.
\tag{F3}\label{eq:source-meet}
\]
Properness gives $U_{\rm meet}\ne\varnothing$.

Now fix one
\[
b\in U_{\rm meet}
\]
inside the proof and keep it fixed before the defect and final grade are
quantified.  Put
\[
H=H_{\beta(b)},
\qquad
d_*=d_0(b).
\tag{F4}\label{eq:source-freeze}
\]
This is ordinary existential elimination of one inhabited source fibre, not a
section depending on the final grade.  Every Tail threshold below belongs to
this same fixed source.

\begin{proposition}[Source--defect tradeoff]
\label{prop:source-defect-tradeoff}
For the present affine provider, the available defect floor tends to infinity
along the moving source filter.  Hence the source cannot be reselected as a
function of the final grade after a fixed defect has been chosen.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Every nontrivial bridge is a Boolean endpoint-switch family containing a
length-three alternative, so its defect supremum is positive.  The Prefix
passes through infinitely many nontrivial bridge stages and defect supremum is
additive under full compatible products.  Hence the floor from which the
subsequent defect-filling cube starts grows without bound.  The generic finite
seed contributes only a finite additional defect at each fixed handoff.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Same-source opposite-coset cancellation}

Fix $d\ge d_*$ at the source \eqref{eq:source-freeze}.  Proposition
\ref{prop:affine-ray-projection} gives
\[
p_F:F_d\twoheadrightarrow q_H^{-1}(a_d)
\]
with constant profile $(m_d,d)$ and branch value in $(0,2-\eta)$.  Use its
complete finite footprint as the transparent obstacle for
Theorem~\ref{thm:ltar-closed}.

Apply that theorem at the same source $H$, target residue
\[
-a_d\in A/H,
\]
and tolerance $\eta/2$.  There is $h_0(d)$ such that for every
$h\ge h_0(d)$ it gives a compatible binary Tail family $T_h$ of profile
$(h,0)$ whose centered-value map
\[
\tau:T_h\to A
\]
satisfies
\[
q_H\tau=-a_d,
\qquad
0\le W(t)<\eta/2
\quad(t\in T_h).
\]
The threshold may depend on the fixed residue and affine obstacle, hence on
$d$, but not on $h$.

Negation factors through the affine coset,
\[
-\tau:T_h\to q_H^{-1}(a_d).
\]
Form the pullback
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
M_{d,h} \arrow[r] \arrow[d,two heads]
& F_d \arrow[d,two heads,"p_F"]\\
T_h \arrow[r,"-\tau"']
& q_H^{-1}(a_d).
\end{tikzcd}
\tag{F5}\label{eq:coset-cancellation-pb}
\]
Every matched pair $(f,t)$ satisfies
\[
[W(f)]=-[W(t)]\quad\text{in }A,
\]
so
\[
[W(f)+W(t)]=0\quad\text{in }\Q/\Z.
\tag{F6}\label{eq:integer-total}
\]
The Tail was constructed against the complete affine footprint, so every
matched pair is physically compatible and $f\sqcup t$ is an actual state.
Moreover
\[
0<W(f\sqcup t)
<2-\eta+\eta/2
<2.
\]
Together with \eqref{eq:integer-total},
\[
W(f\sqcup t)\in\Z\cap(0,2)=\{1\}.
\]
Thus the value is exactly one.

Component profiles add:
\[
(m_d,d)+(h,0)=(m_d+h,d).
\tag{F7}\label{eq:profile-addition-root}
\]
Therefore every
\[
b\ge b_0(d):=m_d+h_0(d)
\]
is realized by taking $h=b-m_d$.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:fibrewise}]
The source $b\in U_{\rm meet}$ was fixed once, before $d$ and the final binary
count were quantified, so $d_*=d_0(b)$ is one finite natural number.  For each
$d\ge d_*$ the preceding construction supplies $b_0(d)$ and realizes every
$b\ge b_0(d)$ with exact value $1$ and component profile $(b,d)$.  This is
Theorem~\ref{thm:fibrewise}.  The proof-local source and all construction
coordinates are forgotten after the existential statement is established.
\end{proof}

For Erdős Problem 289 itself no finite meet is needed: fix one $d\ge d_*$ and
translate the final binary-count ray by $d$.  For every finite
$D\ge d_*$, the finite intersection of the corresponding final total-grade
sets gives the stronger finite-defect uniformity statement.
