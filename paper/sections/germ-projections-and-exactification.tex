\section{Filtered response tails and exactification}
\label{sec:germs}

The root consumes the two responses proved in the preceding sections at one
common endpoint source.  Both are eventual statements on the ordinary
$B\to\infty$ filter on $\N$.  We first project the stronger MasterSlab to one
defect fibre, then freeze one source lying beyond both source thresholds.

\subsection{Projection to the filtered affine ray}

Theorem~\ref{thm:masterslab} gives fixed $c,\eta>0$ and a threshold
$B_{\rm aff}$ such that for every $B\ge B_{\rm aff}$ and every
$L\ge d_0(B)$ there are a quotient point
\[
a_{B,L}\in A/E_B
\]
and a finite homogeneous family $S_{B,L}$ with the literal regular epimorphism
\[
(\operatorname{res},b_3):
S_{B,L}\twoheadrightarrow
q_{E_B}^{-1}(a_{B,L})\times[d_0(B),L].
\tag{F1}\label{eq:slab-root}
\]
All branches lie in $(0,2-\eta)$ and the complete family is
$c$-transparent at the same source $E_B$.

\begin{proposition}[Affine-ray fibre projection]
\label{prop:affine-ray-projection}
For every $B\ge B_{\rm aff}$ and every $d\ge d_0(B)$ there is a finite
physical family
\[
p_F:F_{B,d}\twoheadrightarrow q_{E_B}^{-1}(a_{B,d})
\]
covering one literal affine coset in $A=\Q/\Z$, with constant component profile
\[
(m_{B,d},d),
\]
branchwise reciprocal value in $(0,2-\eta)$, and transparency lower bound $c$
at source $E_B$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Set $L=d$ in \eqref{eq:slab-root} and pull back the regular epimorphism along
\[
q_{E_B}^{-1}(a_{B,d})\times\{d\}
\hookrightarrow
q_{E_B}^{-1}(a_{B,d})\times[d_0(B),d].
\]
Regular epimorphisms are stable under pullback, so the first projection is the
displayed literal affine-coset cover.  The fixed defect fibre has ternary count
$d$ and inherits the common total grade $K_{B,d}$ of the MasterSlab; hence its
constant component profile is
\[
(m_{B,d},d)=(K_{B,d}-d,d).
\]
The branchwise margin and transparency are hereditary under restriction.
All other defect fibres and selector data have reached their last consumer.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Freezing one common source}

Use the affine margin to set
\[
\varepsilon=\eta/2.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:ltar-closed}, with this fixed $c$ and $\varepsilon$, gives a
source threshold $B_{\rm tail}$ that is uniform in every $c$-transparent
finite obstacle and every residue in $A/E_B$.  Choose once, inside the proof,
\[
B\ge\max\{B_{\rm aff},B_{\rm tail}\}
\tag{F2}\label{eq:source-freeze}
\]
and keep this $B$ fixed before the defect and final binary count are quantified.
Put
\[
E:=E_B,
\qquad
d_*:=d_0(B).
\tag{F3}\label{eq:defect-threshold}
\]
No filter choice remains after this line: all later statements are ordinary
statements at the one fixed compact source $E$.

The source cannot subsequently be moved as a function of the requested defect:
the Prefix defect floor generally increases with $B$ because nontrivial bridge
rows contain ternary alternatives.  The source freeze is therefore part of the
logical content, not a notational convenience.

\subsection{Same-source opposite-coset cancellation}

Fix any $d\ge d_*$.  Proposition~\ref{prop:affine-ray-projection} at the frozen
source gives
\[
p_F:F_d\twoheadrightarrow q_E^{-1}(a_d)
\tag{F4}\label{eq:affine-coset-cover}
\]
with constant profile $(m_d,d)$ and branch value in $(0,2-\eta)$.  Regard the
complete finite family $F_d$ as the obstacle for the Tail.  By construction it
is $c$-transparent at $E$.

Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:ltar-closed} at this same source, with target residue
\[
-a_d\in A/E
\]
and tolerance $\eta/2$.  There exists $h_0(d)$ such that for every
$h\ge h_0(d)$ there is an obstacle-compatible binary family $T_h$ of profile
$(h,0)$.  Write
\[
\tau(t)=[W(t)]\in A.
\]
Then
\[
q_E\tau=-a_d,
\qquad
0\le W(t)<\eta/2
\quad(t\in T_h).
\tag{F5}\label{eq:tail-opposite-residue}
\]
The threshold may depend on the fixed affine obstacle and residue, hence on
$d$, but not on $h$.

Negation factors canonically through the affine coset,
\[
-\tau:T_h\to q_E^{-1}(a_d).
\]
Form the literal pullback
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
M_{d,h} \arrow[r] \arrow[d,two heads]
& F_d \arrow[d,two heads,"p_F"]\\
T_h \arrow[r,"-\tau"']
& q_E^{-1}(a_d).
\end{tikzcd}
\tag{F6}\label{eq:coset-cancellation-pb}
\]
The left projection is surjective by pullback stability.  Every matched pair
$(f,t)$ satisfies
\[
[W(f)]=-[W(t)]\quad\text{in }A,
\]
and hence
\[
[W(f)+W(t)]=0\quad\text{in }\Q/\Z.
\tag{F7}\label{eq:integer-total}
\]
The Tail was constructed against the complete affine obstacle, so the pair is
physically compatible and $f\sqcup t$ is an actual state.  Moreover
\[
0<W(f\sqcup t)
<2-\eta+\eta/2
<2.
\]
Together with \eqref{eq:integer-total}, this gives
\[
W(f\sqcup t)\in\Z\cap(0,2)=\{1\}.
\]
Thus the reciprocal value is exactly one.

Component profiles add:
\[
(m_d,d)+(h,0)=(m_d+h,d).
\tag{F8}\label{eq:profile-addition-root}
\]
Therefore every binary count
\[
b\ge b_0(d):=m_d+h_0(d)
\]
is realized by taking $h=b-m_d$.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:fibrewise}]
The endpoint source $B$ in \eqref{eq:source-freeze} was fixed once before $d$
and the final binary count were quantified, so $d_*=d_0(B)$ is one finite
natural number.  For every $d\ge d_*$ the preceding construction supplies a
finite threshold $b_0(d)$ and realizes every $b\ge b_0(d)$ with reciprocal
value exactly $1$ and component profile $(b,d)$.  This is precisely
Theorem~\ref{thm:fibrewise}.
\end{proof}

For Erdős Problem 289 itself no finite meet is required: fix one $d\ge d_*$
and translate the final binary-count ray by $d$.  Finite intersections over
$d\in[d_*,D]$ give the stronger finite-defect uniformity statement.
