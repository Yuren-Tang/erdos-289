\section{Finite realization by compatible intervals}
\label{sec:finite-realization}

We now return from the component-count argument to literal intervals.  Haxell's theorem is used only after a finite cutoff has been fixed.

Let $\eta>0$ be the reciprocal-mass margin in Proposition~\ref{prop:wide-start}.  By \eqref{eq:role-summable}, choose $B_{\mathrm{mass}}$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:resource-tail}
\sum_{Q>B_{\mathrm{mass}}}\omega_Q<\eta.
\end{equation}
Choose also $B_{\mathrm{pack}}$ so large that every compatible subfamily $\mathcal A_Q(B)$ occurring after that point has cardinality at least the quota denominator $L_P$ of Proposition~\ref{prop:haxell-quota}.  This is possible by \eqref{eq:family-size} and Proposition~\ref{prop:wide-start}.  Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:scalar-terminality} with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:global-start-cutoff}
B_\dagger=\max\{B_{\mathrm{mass}},B_{\mathrm{pack}}\}.
\end{equation}

Fix $X\in\mathcal H_B$.  Only finitely many prime-power steps lie between $B$ and $X$.  For each such $Q$, Proposition~\ref{prop:wide-start} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:survivor-density}
|\mathcal A_Q(B)|
\ge\sigma|\mathcal A_Q|
\ge\kappa\sigma\frac Q{\log Q}.
\end{equation}
The classes $\mathcal A_Q(B)$ are pairwise disjoint: by the construction in Section~\ref{sec:signed-inverse}, a retained interval uniquely recovers its top prime-power stage $Q$.  Join two members of their finite union when they are incompatible as interval components.  Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-inverse-capabilities} gives a uniform maximum degree.  Proposition~\ref{prop:haxell-quota}, applied to this partition, yields an independent set $P_X$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:packed-density}
|P_X\cap\mathcal A_Q(B)|
\ge\rho_P|\mathcal A_Q(B)|
>\rho\frac Q{\log Q}.
\end{equation}
Hence
\[
P_X(Q):=P_X\cap\mathcal A_Q(B)
\in\mathcal D_\rho(Q)
\]
for every step through $X$.

\subsection{From the simple quotient to the affine fibre}

The local theorem concerns $S_Q=F_Q/F_{<Q}$, while the global induction concerns affine fibres in $A=\Q/\Z$.  The connection is the pullback square
\begin{equation}\label{eq:simple-factor-pullback}
\begin{CD}
F_Q @>>> A\\
@V{q}VV @VV{q_{F_{<Q}}}V\\
S_Q=F_Q/F_{<Q} @>>> A/F_{<Q}
\end{CD}
\end{equation}
where the bottom arrow is the natural inclusion.

\begin{lemma}[Affine lifting across one prime-power step]\label{lem:affine-current-lift}
Let $Q$ be a prime power, and let
\[
R:X_0\rightsquigarrow
\operatorname{Tor}_{F_{<Q}}(\alpha)\times I
\]
be a covering labelled relation, with $\alpha\in A/F_{<Q}$.  Let $\bar\alpha\in A/F_Q$ be the image of $\alpha$.  Suppose a finite local labelled relation has residues in $F_Q$ and, for every $s\in S_Q$ and $k'\in I'$, contains an edge labelled by $L$ together with some $i\in I$ satisfying
\[
[\overline W(L)]_{S_Q}=s,
\qquad i+\nu(L)=k'.
\]
If the incoming and local labels are jointly compatible, their composite is covering onto
\[
\operatorname{Tor}_{F_Q}(\bar\alpha)\times I'.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix $(y,k')$ in the target fibre and choose $x_0\in\operatorname{Tor}_{F_{<Q}}(\alpha)$.  Since $y$ and $x_0$ have the same image in $A/F_Q$, their difference belongs to $F_Q$.  Let $s$ be its class in $S_Q$, and choose $L$ and $i$ with simple class $s$ and $i+\nu(L)=k'$.  The pullback property \eqref{eq:simple-factor-pullback} gives
\[
y-\overline W(L)-x_0\in F_{<Q}.
\]
Thus $y-\overline W(L)$ lies in the incoming affine fibre.  An incoming edge ending there, composed with the chosen local edge, ends at $(y,k')$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Joint compatibility and exact composition}

The local relations in Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-profile} use finite subsets of the dense restriction $P_X(Q)$.

\begin{lemma}[Joint compatibility]\label{lem:joint-compatibility}
Let $S_0$ be a label of the affine initial cover.  Let $Q_1,\ldots,Q_m\le X$ be distinct later prime-power steps, and for each $i$ let $L_i$ be the union of a finite subset of $P_X(Q_i)$.  Then
\[
(S_0,L_1,\ldots,L_m)\in D_{m+1}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
All interval components selected from the sets $P_X(Q_i)$ belong to the single independent set $P_X$, so they are pairwise disjoint and nonadjacent.  Each lies in $\mathcal A_{Q_i}(B)$ and is therefore compatible with every initial-cover label, in particular with $S_0$.  Their literal union with $S_0$ is consequently an element of $\mathcal C$.
\end{proof}

Let $P_n$ be the fibre product of the edge sets in the finite chain through $X$.  Every local edge label is a union of a finite subset of one $P_X(Q)$, so Lemma~\ref{lem:joint-compatibility} gives the factorization
\begin{equation}\label{eq:realization-factorization}
\begin{CD}
P_n @>{\ell}>> \mathcal C^n\\
@V{\widetilde\ell}VV @AA{\iota}A\\
D_n @= D_n
\end{CD}
\end{equation}
Hence the comparison $2$-cell of \eqref{eq:oplax-2cell} is an identity by \eqref{eq:rel-strictification}.  At each prime-power step, Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-profile} gives the simple-quotient rectangle and Lemma~\ref{lem:affine-current-lift} lifts it to the next endpoint fibre.  Finite induction therefore produces a finite set $X_0(X)$ and a covering labelled relation
\[
R_X:X_0(X)\rightsquigarrow T_X,
\]
where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:finite-response}
T_X:=\operatorname{Tor}_{E_X}(\gamma_X)\times I_X
\end{equation}
and $\gamma_X$ is the image of $\gamma_B$ under the endpoint quotient map.

\subsection{Reciprocal-mass control}

If $S_0$ is a label in the affine initial cover, Proposition~\ref{prop:wide-start} gives $W(S_0)<2-\eta$.  Each later prime-power step contributes reciprocal mass at most $\omega_Q$.  By \eqref{eq:mass-subadditive} and \eqref{eq:resource-tail}, every interval configuration $S$ labelling an edge of $R_X$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-W-less-2}
W(S)
\le W(S_0)+\sum_{B<Q\le X}\omega_Q
<2.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Finite realization through a cutoff]\label{prop:finite-realization}
For every $X\in\mathcal H_B$ there are a finite set $X_0(X)$ and a labelled relation
\[
R_X:X_0(X)\rightsquigarrow T_X
\]
whose target projection is surjective, where $T_X$ is given by \eqref{eq:finite-response}.  Every edge label $S$ of $R_X$ satisfies $W(S)<2$.
\end{proposition}
