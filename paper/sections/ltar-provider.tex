% R14 direct targeted Tail provider.

\section{The direct source-filtered Tail provider}
\label{sec:ltar-provider}

The Tail root consumes one object only: a finite binary physical family of
prescribed grade whose residue hits one prescribed endpoint-source coset and
whose branchwise reciprocal resource is small.  We construct it directly from
local homogeneous simple covers.

\subsection{Filtration and transverse raw rows}

Put
\[
A=\Q/\Z.
\]
For a prime power $Q=p^e$ let
\[
J_Q=\ker([Q]:A\to A),
\]
and define
\[
F_{<Q}=\bigvee_{R<Q,\ R\ {\rm prime\ power}}J_R,
\qquad
F_Q=F_{<Q}\vee J_Q,
\qquad
S_Q=F_Q/F_{<Q}.
\]
The new $p$-primary height is one step, so $S_Q$ has order $p$.  We call $Q$ a
\emph{current}.  A binary atom is filtered-transverse at $Q$ when its centered
residue lies in $F_Q$ and has nonzero image in $S_Q$.

The signed-inverse construction supplies the physical raw rows.  Fix constants
$1<B_0<B_1$ and let the carrier prime $b$ range in a fixed comparable interval
\[
Q/B_1<b<Q/B_0.
\]
For a sufficiently large current $Q=p^e$ these carriers are different from
$p$.  Let $c_+$ be the inverse of $b$ modulo $Q$, put $c_-=Q-c_+$, and write
\[
bc_+=Qk_++1,
\qquad
bc_-=Qk_--1.
\tag{T1}\label{eq:signed-inverse-r14}
\]
Then
\[
k_++k_-=b,
\qquad
1\le k_\pm<b<Q,
\]
so at least one $k_\sigma$ is a $p$-unit.

For each sign, only $O(1)$ carriers fail filtered downwardness.  Indeed the
only way the companion denominator $bc_\sigma$ can acquire a maximal
prime-power factor not below $Q$ is that the carrier prime $b$ divides
$c_\sigma$.  Then
\[
c_\sigma=\lambda b,
\qquad 1\le\lambda<B_1,
\]
and the inverse relation gives
\[
\lambda b^2\equiv\sigma1\pmod Q.
\]
For each fixed $(\lambda,\sigma)$ this unit quadratic congruence has uniformly
boundedly many roots modulo the prime power $Q$, and the carrier interval has
length $<Q$.  Deleting the two finite exceptional sets leaves every admissible
$p$-unit orientation filtered downward.  The consecutive pair
\[
\{Qk_\sigma,Qk_\sigma+\sigma1\}
\]
is then a filtered-transverse binary atom.

Its simple value is intrinsic.  If $u k_\sigma\equiv1\pmod p$, then
\[
\frac1{Qk_\sigma}-\frac uQ
=
\frac{1-u k_\sigma}{Qk_\sigma}
\in F_{<Q},
\]
because the numerator removes one $p$-height and every prime-power factor of
$k_\sigma$ is below $Q$.  The companion reciprocal is also in $F_{<Q}$ by the
downwardness deletion.  Hence the simple-factor image is the nonzero class
$u[1/Q]\in S_Q$.

A fixed coefficient $k$ comes from only $O(1)$ carrier/orientation pairs.  For
such a pair the carrier prime divides one of
\[
Qk-1,\qquad Qk+1,
\]
which are $<Q^2+1$.  Since every carrier is $>Q/B_1$, the product of more than
a fixed number of distinct carriers would exceed $Q^2+1$.  This proves bounded
coefficient multiplicity.

Comparable-prime supply from Appendix~\ref{app:prime-supply}, followed by the
finite deletions and bounded-multiplicity thinning, gives
\[
|R_Q|\gg Q/\log Q.
\tag{T2}\label{eq:raw-row-size}
\]
The physical envelopes have uniformly bounded span and globally injective
distinguished centres.

For a generic proper current retain the upper half of the distinct usable
coefficients.  Then $k\gg Q/\log Q$, so the atom has branchwise reciprocal
mass
\[
O(\log Q/Q^2).
\]
At a prime current $Q=p$, both $k_+$ and $k_-$ are $p$-units.  After the
simultaneous downwardness deletion take
\[
k^*=\max(k_+,k_-).
\]
Since $k_++k_-=b\asymp p$, bounded coefficient multiplicity leaves
$\gg p/\log p$ distinct coefficients with $k^*\asymp p$, and their atoms have
mass $O(p^{-2})$.  Thus
\[
O(p^{-2})\quad(Q=p),
\qquad
O(\log Q/Q^2)\quad(Q=p^e,\ e\ge2)
\tag{T3}\label{eq:raw-resource}
\]
are valid proof-local resource bounds.  At a prime current the retained simple
values are distinct.  At $Q=p^2$, a fixed simple value fixes $k$ modulo $p$;
there are at most $p$ such integers $1\le k<p^2$, and bounded coefficient
multiplicity therefore gives a simple-value fibre of size $O(p)$.

Call a finite obstacle $c$-transparent at an eligible endpoint source $E_B$
when, for every active current beyond that source and every boundary
predecessor required by the first higher current, deleting all raw atoms
conflicting with the obstacle leaves at least a fixed fraction $c>0$ of the
row.  After this deletion the conflict graph has uniformly bounded maximum
degree.  Apply the quota-packing consequence of Haxell's theorem from
Appendix~\ref{app:packing} once to the finite rows below a chosen endpoint.
The resulting local pools are disjoint subsets of one globally independent
set.  Hence all later matched unions are physical, and packing loses only
another fixed positive fraction of every sufficiently large row.

\subsection{The common local interface}

Let
\[
F^-\le F,
\qquad S=F/F^-
\]
be one prime-order simple step.  A \emph{homogeneous simple cover} of grade
$m$ and resource $\rho$ is a finite binary physical family $X$ such that every
branch is obstacle-compatible, has grade exactly $m$ and resource at most
$\rho$, its observation lies in $F$, and
\[
X\longrightarrow S
\]
is surjective.  No construction coordinate survives this interface.

\subsection{Width-bearing prime currents}

Let $Q=p$ be prime.  After obstacle deletion and global packing, the simple
value image contains a set
\[
X_p\subset S_p\cong\mathbf F_p,
\qquad
N_p:=|X_p|\gg p/\log p
\]
of distinct points.  The dense convolution theorem of
Appendix~\ref{app:atomic-convolution} applies.  Choose once a proof-local
constant $C$ beyond its threshold and define
\[
\boxed{
m_p:=\left\lceil C(\log p)^3\right\rceil.}
\tag{T4}\label{eq:mp-definition}
\]
For every fixed $L>1$, eventually
\[
Lm_p\le N_p/2,
\]
and every
\[
M\in[m_p,Lm_p]
\tag{T5}\label{eq:atomic-grade-interval}
\]
has a homogeneous simple cover.  Pulling the $M$ disjoint two-point slots from
the convolution theorem back to packed physical atoms gives a family every
branch of which contains exactly one atom from each slot and therefore has
grade $M$.  Uniformly on this interval its resource is
\[
O((\log p)^4/p^2).
\tag{T6}\label{eq:atomic-resource}
\]

\subsection{Proper prime powers}

We use one elementary prime-cyclic fact.  Translate $p-1$ two-point slots to
$\{0,d_i\}$ with $d_i\ne0$.  Starting from $A_0=\{0\}$, put
\[
A_i=A_{i-1}\cup(A_{i-1}+d_i).
\]
If $A_{i-1}\ne\mathbf F_p$, then $A_{i-1}+d_i\ne A_{i-1}$, because one
nonzero translation generates the cyclic group.  Hence the support grows by at
least one until it is all of $\mathbf F_p$.  Thus $p-1$ mutually compatible
same-grade two-point slots with nonzero simple difference form a homogeneous
simple cover.

At $Q=p^2$, the packed row has $\gg p^2/\log p$ atoms and every simple-value
fibre has size $O(p)$.  After finitely many exceptions one can therefore choose
$p-1$ disjoint pairs with unequal simple values.  They form a cover of common
grade $p-1$ and resource
\[
O(\log p/p^3).
\tag{T7}\label{eq:square-resource}
\]

For $Q=p^e$, $e\ge3$, choose $p-1$ unused current atoms and $p-1$ unused atoms
from the immediate predecessor row $p^{e-1}$.  Predecessor observations lie in
the source of the new simple quotient and are current-neutral, whereas every
current atom has nonzero simple value.  Pairing them gives $p-1$ same-grade
slots and hence a full simple cover of grade $p-1$.  The demand is only
$O(p)$ against row size $\gg p^e/(e\log p)$.  If the predecessor straddles the
source boundary, source-closed transparency includes exactly this boundary
row, whose index escapes to infinity with the source.  A summable majorant is
\[
O\!\left(\frac{e\log p}{p^{2e-3}}\right)
\qquad(e\ge3).
\tag{T8}\label{eq:deep-resource}
\]

Above this subsection every proper prime power is represented only by a
homogeneous simple cover of grade $p-1$.

\subsection{Target-specific filtered lifting}

Let
\[
G_0\le G_1\le\cdots\le G_n
\]
be a finite source-to-endpoint filtration with prime-order factors
$S_i=G_i/G_{i-1}$.  Let
\[
v_i:X_i\to G_i
\]
be homogeneous simple-cover families such that $X_i\to S_i$ is surjective.

\begin{lemma}[Targeted filtered lifting]\label{lem:targeted-filtered-lifting}
For every generalized target
\[
r:T\to G_n/G_0,
\]
the object of tuples $(x_n,\ldots,x_1)$ satisfying
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n v_i(x_i)=r\pmod{G_0}
\tag{T9}\label{eq:targeted-match}
\]
projects surjectively to $T$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Project $r$ to the last simple quotient and pull back the surjection
$X_n\to S_n$.  Retain the full pullback fibre.  For an actual branch $x_n$,
the difference $r-v_n(x_n)$ lands canonically in $G_{n-1}/G_0$.  Repeat
through the filtration.  Every step is a pullback of a surjection, hence the
finite composite is surjective; at the bottom the residual lies in $G_0$.
No splitting or representative section is chosen.
\end{proof}

Because all $X_i$ lie in one globally packed universe, a matched tuple maps by
literal physical union to a binary family.  Local grades and branchwise
resource bounds add.

\subsection{Grade finality}

Fix a sufficiently late eligible source index $B$ and a finite numerical
endpoint $X>B$.  For each prime current choose independently
\[
M_p\in[m_p,Lm_p],
\]
while every proper prime power contributes its fixed resolver grade $p-1$.
The exact image of the local-grade addition map is one integer interval
\[
[A_B(X),B_{B,L}(X)]\cap\N.
\tag{T10}\label{eq:r14-grade-interval}
\]
No grade tuple is selected: for a requested total grade we retain the whole
addition fibre.

Comparable-prime supply gives
\[
\sum_{B<p\le X}m_p\asymp X(\log X)^2
\tag{T11}\label{eq:mp-sum}
\]
for $X$ sufficiently beyond the fixed source.  Proper prime powers add only a
lower-order shift: squares contribute $O(X)$, and exponents $e\ge3$ contribute
$O(X^{2/3}\log X)$.

Let $\Lambda>1$ be the fixed endpoint dilation from comparable-prime supply.
For one constant $C_1$,
\[
A_B(\Lambda X)-A_B(X)
\le C_1X(\log X)^2
\]
eventually, whereas
\[
B_{B,L}(X)-A_B(X)
=(L-1)\sum_{B<p\le X}m_p
\ge c_1(L-1)X(\log X)^2.
\]
Choose $L$ once with $c_1(L-1)>C_1$.  Then
\[
A_B(\Lambda X)\le B_{B,L}(X)+1
\tag{T12}\label{eq:r14-overlap}
\]
eventually.  Consecutive endpoint intervals overlap or are adjacent and their
lower endpoints tend to infinity; their union is final in $\N$.

\subsection{Resource decay}

The prime-current majorant has convergent sum
\[
\sum_p\frac{(\log p)^4}{p^2}<\infty.
\]
The square and higher-power majorants are dominated by convergent
integer/geometric series.  Boundary-predecessor terms form a subseries whose
indices escape every fixed finite set as the eligible source advances.  Hence
the total branchwise resource beyond source $E_B$ is the tail of one convergent
positive series and tends to zero along the eligible-source filter.

\subsection{Direct late-source targeted residue response}

Let $E_B$ be a sufficiently late eligible source, let $O$ be a
$c$-transparent finite obstacle there, and let
\[
r\in A/E_B.
\]
Since $A/E_B$ is torsion, choose a proof-local finite endpoint $E_X\ge E_B$
whose relative quotient contains $r$, and start the geometric endpoint
sequence beyond $X$.  For each total grade in the final interval, retain the
full local-grade-tuple fibre and then apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:targeted-filtered-lifting} to the same local physical families.
The resulting matched family has source residue exactly $r$ and carries the
same branchwise resource bound.

\begin{theorem}[Direct late-source targeted saturation]\label{thm:ltar-closed}
Fix $c>0$ and $\varepsilon>0$.  Eventually in the eligible endpoint index $B$,
for every $c$-transparent finite obstacle $O$, every residue
\[
r\in A/E_B,
\]
and every sufficiently large grade $h$, there is a finite obstacle-compatible
binary physical family of component profile $(h,0)$, source residue $r$, and
branchwise reciprocal resource $<\varepsilon$.
\end{theorem}

The source-valid locus is uniform in $O$ and $r$.  After $B$ is fixed, the
grade threshold may depend on $O$ and $r$ through the proof-local endpoint and
finite packing problem.  This is exactly the quantifier order forced by the
fixed-source mesh obstruction.
