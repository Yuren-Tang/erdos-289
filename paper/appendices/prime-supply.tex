\section{Comparable prime supply}
\label{app:prime-supply}

The proof needs only one fixed-ratio prime band with two-sided order of
magnitude $X/\log X$.  We record an elementary Chebyshev proof.

Put
\[
\theta(x)=\sum_{p\le x}\log p,
\qquad
\psi(x)=\sum_{p^m\le x}\log p
=\log\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,\lfloor x\rfloor).
\]
The central binomial coefficient satisfies
\[
\binom{2n}{n}\mid\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,2n+1).
\]
Indeed, for each prime $p$, Legendre's formula gives
\[
v_p\binom{2n}{n}
=\sum_{j\ge1}
\left(\left\lfloor\frac{2n}{p^j}\right\rfloor
-2\left\lfloor\frac n{p^j}\right\rfloor\right),
\]
and every summand is $0$ or $1$; if the sum is $e$, then some nonzero summand
has index at least $e$, so $p^e\le2n$.

Since the central binomial coefficient is maximal in its row,
\[
4^n\le(2n+1)\binom{2n}{n},
\]
hence
\[
\psi(x)\ge c_\psi x
\]
eventually.  On the other hand, every prime $n<p\le2n$ divides
$\binom{2n}{n}$, so dyadic telescoping gives
\[
\theta(x)\le C_\theta x.
\]
Because
\[
\psi(x)=\sum_{m\ge1}\theta(x^{1/m}),
\]
the higher-power contribution is $O(\sqrt x\log x)=o(x)$, and therefore
\[
c_\theta x\le\theta(x)\le C_\theta x
\tag{P1}\label{eq:chebyshev-linear}
\]
eventually.

Choose one fixed $\Lambda>1$ with
$C_\theta<c_\theta\Lambda$.  Then
\[
\theta(\Lambda X)-\theta(X)
\ge(c_\theta\Lambda-C_\theta)X,
\]
while every prime in $(X,\Lambda X]$ contributes between
$\log X$ and $\log(\Lambda X)$.  Thus:

\begin{proposition}[Comparable prime band]\label{prop:prime-band}
For some fixed $\Lambda>1$ and constants $c,C>0$,
\[
c\frac X{\log X}
\le
\#\{p:X<p\le\Lambda X\}
\le
C\frac X{\log X}
\]
for every sufficiently large $X$.
\end{proposition}

No numerical value of $\Lambda,c,C$ survives the provider boundary.
