\section{Finite physical realization}
\label{sec:finite-realization}

This section is where simultaneous geometric compatibility is imposed.  After a finite cutoff $X$ has been fixed, Haxell's theorem produces a common independent family over the finitely many active prime-power rows.  On that family the path-label map factors through the joint-compatibility domain, so the compatible covering theorem pastes the local transfers to a covering physical correspondence.

Let
\[
0<\eta<\eta_0,
\qquad
0<\sigma<1,
\qquad
0<\rho<\kappa\sigma\rho_P
\]
be arbitrary parameters as in Sections~\ref{sec:wide-start}--\ref{sec:scalar-finality}.  Define the reciprocal-mass and packing cutoff objects
\[
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{mass}}(\eta)
=
\left\{B:\sum_{Q>B}\omega_Q<\eta\right\},
\]
\[
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{pack}}(\sigma)
=
\left\{B:
\kappa\sigma\frac Q{\log Q}\ge L_P
\text{ for every prime power }Q>B
\right\}.
\]
By \eqref{eq:role-summable} and $Q/\log Q\to\infty$, both contain final segments of $\N$.  The physical cutoff object is their intersection with the continuation object:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:global-start-cutoff}
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{phys}}(\eta,\sigma,\rho)
=
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{mass}}(\eta)
\cap
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{pack}}(\sigma)
\cap
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{cont}}(\rho).
\end{equation}
It too contains a final segment.  Thus there is a common onset $B_*(\eta,\sigma,\rho)$ beyond which the continuation, packing and reciprocal-mass requirements all hold.

Let $B\in\mathcal B_{\mathrm{phys}}(\eta,\sigma,\rho)$ and let
\[
\xi\in\mathfrak W_B(\eta,\sigma,L_\rho)
\]
be arbitrary.  The argument below is fibrewise in $\xi$.  Write $R_B^\xi,\gamma_B^\xi,I_B^\xi$ for its initial data and $\mathcal A_Q(\xi)$ for its surviving later families.

\subsection{The Haxell parameter set}

For $X\ge B$, only finitely many prime-power steps lie between $B$ and $X$.  For every such $Q$, Proposition~\ref{prop:wide-start} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:survivor-density}
|\mathcal A_Q(\xi)|
\ge\sigma|\mathcal A_Q|
\ge\kappa\sigma\frac Q{\log Q}.
\end{equation}
The classes $\mathcal A_Q(\xi)$ are pairwise disjoint by Lemma~\ref{lem:top-stage-recovery}.  Let $G_{X,\xi}$ be the incompatibility graph on their finite union, partitioned by these classes.  By the definition of $\Delta_*$ in Section~\ref{sec:signed-inverse}, the graph has maximum degree at most $\Delta_*$.  Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:haxell-parameter-object}
\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}
=
\operatorname{IQ}_{L_P}
\bigl(G_{X,\xi};(\mathcal A_Q(\xi))_{B<Q\le X}\bigr).
\end{equation}
By Proposition~\ref{prop:haxell-quota},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:haxell-parameter-nonempty}
\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}\ne\varnothing.
\end{equation}
Every $P\in\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:packed-density}
|P\cap\mathcal A_Q(\xi)|
\ge\rho_P|\mathcal A_Q(\xi)|
>\rho\frac Q{\log Q}.
\end{equation}
Hence
\[
P(Q):=P\cap\mathcal A_Q(\xi)
\in\mathcal D_\rho(Q)
\]
for every prime-power step $B<Q\le X$.

\subsection{Local affine translation at one prime-power step}

At a prime-power step $Q$, put
\[
H=F_{<Q},
\qquad
K=F_Q.
\]
The general exact sequence \eqref{eq:affine-extension-exact} specializes to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:simple-extension}
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=small]
0 \arrow[r] & S_Q \arrow[r] & A/F_{<Q} \arrow[r] & A/F_Q \arrow[r] & 0,
\end{tikzcd}
\end{equation}
and
\[
\operatorname{Fib}_{\pi_{F_{<Q},F_Q}}(0)=S_Q.
\]

For $P\in\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}$ define the finite local witness object
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-local-labels}
\mathscr O_P(Q)
=
\{O\subseteq P(Q):|O|\le p\}
\end{equation}
with label map
\[
\lambda_{Q,P}:\mathscr O_P(Q)\longrightarrow\mathcal C,
\qquad
O\longmapsto\bigcup_{I\in O}I.
\]
Because $P$ is independent, every displayed union belongs to $\mathcal C$.  By \eqref{eq:signed-label-filtration}, its residue lies in $F_Q$, and its image in $S_Q\times\N$ is exactly the point of
\[
\Gamma_{\phi_Q|_{P(Q)}}
\]
represented by the same subset $O$.  Thus Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-profile} states precisely that the map \eqref{eq:graded-quotient-regular-epi}, with $\beta=0$, is a regular epimorphism onto the scalar target rectangle at this step.  Corollary~\ref{cor:graded-affine-translation} therefore turns the abstract quotient support into a covering labelled correspondence.

Write
\[
B<Q_1<\cdots<Q_m\le X
\]
for the possibly empty list of processed prime-power steps.

\begin{lemma}[Endpoint ladder]\label{lem:endpoint-ladder}
If $m>0$, then
\[
E_B=F_{<Q_1},
\qquad
E_{Q_j}=F_{Q_j}=F_{<Q_{j+1}}\quad(1\le j<m),
\qquad
E_X=F_{Q_m}.
\]
Consequently the quotient used by the $Q_j$-local affine translation is exactly the consecutive endpoint quotient from the preceding target to the next one.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By definition $Q_1$ is the first prime-power rank strictly larger than $B$, so the prime-power stages of rank at most $B$ are exactly those of rank $<Q_1$, giving $E_B=F_{<Q_1}$.  Likewise no prime-power rank lies strictly between consecutive $Q_j,Q_{j+1}$, hence $F_{Q_j}=F_{<Q_{j+1}}$.  Finally no prime-power rank lies in $(Q_m,X]$, so $E_X=F_{Q_m}$.  The identities $E_{Q_j}=F_{Q_j}$ are \eqref{eq:endpoint-current-step}.
\end{proof}

The successive affine translations therefore paste along the endpoint ladder
\begin{equation}\label{eq:endpoint-ladder-diagram}
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=small]
E_B=F_{<Q_1} \arrow[r] & E_{Q_1}=F_{Q_1} \arrow[r] & \cdots \arrow[r] & E_{Q_m}=F_{Q_m}=E_X,
\end{tikzcd}
\end{equation}
with $F_{Q_j}=F_{<Q_{j+1}}$ at every intermediate junction.

Put
\[
T_0^\xi
=\operatorname{Fib}_{E_B}(\gamma_B^\xi)\times I_B^\xi,
\]
and for $1\le j\le m$ let
\[
\gamma_j^\xi=\pi_{B,Q_j}(\gamma_B^\xi),
\qquad
T_j^\xi
=\operatorname{Fib}_{E_{Q_j}}(\gamma_j^\xi)\times I_{Q_j}^\xi.
\]
For each $P\in\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}$ and $1\le j\le m$, Corollary~\ref{cor:graded-affine-translation} gives a covering labelled correspondence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-local-translation-chain}
\mathscr T_{j,P}:T_{j-1}^\xi\rightsquigarrow T_j^\xi.
\end{equation}
The filtration and scalar interval change only at prime-power steps, so $T_m^\xi=T_X^\xi$ when $m>0$, while $T_0^\xi=T_X^\xi$ when $m=0$.

\subsection{Joint compatibility and strict pasting}

\begin{lemma}[Joint compatibility]\label{lem:joint-compatibility}
For every $P\in\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}$, the product label map formed from an edge label of $R_B^\xi$ and arbitrary local witnesses
\[
O_j\in\mathscr O_P(Q_j)
\qquad(1\le j\le m)
\]
lands in $D_{m+1}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
All interval components occurring in the local labels belong to the single independent set $P$, hence are pairwise disjoint and nonadjacent.  Each belongs to some $\mathcal A_{Q_j}(\xi)$ and is therefore compatible with every component in the prepared-start pool $\mathscr P_B^\xi$, which contains every component of every label of $R_B^\xi$.  Thus every product label is jointly compatible and its literal union lies in $\mathcal C$.
\end{proof}

Put $n=m+1$.  For $P\in\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}$ let $P_n(P)$ be the path pullback of the edge objects in
\[
R_B^\xi,\mathscr T_{1,P},\ldots,\mathscr T_{m,P}.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:joint-compatibility} gives the factorization
\begin{equation}\label{eq:realization-factorization}
\begin{tikzcd}
P_n(P) \arrow[r,"{\widetilde\ell_P}"] & D_n \arrow[r] & \mathcal C^n,
\end{tikzcd}
\qquad
\ell_P=\iota\circ\widetilde\ell_P.
\end{equation}
Hence the compatible path pullback is canonically isomorphic to the full path pullback, and \eqref{eq:rel-strictification} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:observable-master-composite}
U(R_{X,\xi,P})
=
U(\mathscr T_{m,P})\circ\cdots\circ
U(\mathscr T_{1,P})\circ U(R_B^\xi),
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-master-composite}
R_{X,\xi,P}
:=
\mathscr T_{m,P}\odot\cdots\odot
\mathscr T_{1,P}\odot R_B^\xi
:\{0_M\}\rightsquigarrow T_X^\xi.
\end{equation}
Every factor is covering, and \eqref{eq:realization-factorization} is precisely the remaining hypothesis of Theorem~\ref{thm:compatible-covering}.  That theorem makes $R_{X,\xi,P}$ covering by pullback stability.  Equation~\eqref{eq:observable-master-composite} records the corresponding equality after passage to regular images.

Define the physical composite over the Haxell parameter set by the finite coproduct
\begin{equation}\label{eq:universal-physical-composite}
R_{X,\xi}^{\mathrm{univ}}
=
\coprod_{P\in\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}}R_{X,\xi,P}.
\end{equation}
Because \eqref{eq:haxell-parameter-nonempty} is nonempty and every summand is covering, the target map
\begin{equation}\label{eq:universal-realization-epi}
t_{X,\xi}:E_{R_{X,\xi}^{\mathrm{univ}}}
\twoheadrightarrow T_X^\xi
\end{equation}
is a regular epimorphism.

\subsection{Reciprocal-mass control}

Every label of $R_B^\xi$ has reciprocal value below $2-\eta$.  Every local label at step $Q=p^e$ is a compatible union of at most $p$ members of $\mathcal A_Q$, so Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-inverse-capabilities} bounds its reciprocal mass by $\omega_Q$.  Since $B\in\mathcal B_{\mathrm{mass}}(\eta)$, \eqref{eq:mass-subadditive} gives, uniformly in $P\in\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-W-less-2}
W(S)
<2-\eta+\sum_{Q>B}\omega_Q
<2
\end{equation}
for every edge label $S$ of $R_{X,\xi,P}$, and hence for every edge label of the coproduct \eqref{eq:universal-physical-composite}.

\begin{proposition}[Finite realization through a cutoff]\label{prop:finite-realization}
Let $B\in\mathcal B_{\mathrm{phys}}(\eta,\sigma,\rho)$, let
\[
\xi\in\mathfrak W_B(\eta,\sigma,L_\rho),
\qquad
X\ge B.
\]
Then the Haxell parameter set $\mathfrak P_{X,\xi}$ is nonempty and the coproduct correspondence
\[
R_{X,\xi}^{\mathrm{univ}}:\{0_M\}\rightsquigarrow T_X^\xi,
\qquad
T_X^\xi
=\operatorname{Fib}_{E_X}(\gamma_X^\xi)\times I_X^\xi,
\]
is covering.  Every edge label $S$ satisfies $W(S)<2$.
\end{proposition}
